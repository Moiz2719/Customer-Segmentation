# Findings status

After running the project locally, use the generated files under outputs/ to document:

- the selected number of clusters and silhouette score;
- each segment's size and percentage of customers;
- average income, spending, purchase recency, and channel behaviour by segment;
- differences in campaign-response rates between segments;
- recommended business actions and how their impact could be measured.

Avoid naming segments based solely on cluster IDs. Cluster IDs are arbitrary labels and may change if the data or settings change.
